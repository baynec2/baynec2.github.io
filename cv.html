Research

Graduate researcher, UC San Diego

2021 to present
  • Built Conduit, an open-source DIA metaproteomics ecosystem: a Snakemake workflow that scales from one machine to SLURM clusters, a cross-platform desktop launcher, a Shiny analysis app and an R package.
  • Wrote and published mspms, a Bioconductor package and Shiny app for protease substrate profiling by mass spectrometry.
  • Led a plasma proteomics and metabolomics study of enterococcal bacteremia (105 samples), finding host-response signatures of infection type and survival; first author in mSystems.
  • Automated mass spectrometer quality control with FragPipe, SQLite and a Shiny dashboard.
  • Set up the lab’s Oxford Nanopore sequencing, including the basecalling workstation and a bacterial genome assembly protocol.

Kaleido Biosciences, Lexington, MA

2016 to 2021
Senior Research Associate (2020 to 2021), Research Associate II (2018 to 2020), Research Associate (2016 to 2018)
  • Developed therapeutics designed to modulate the gut microbiome, working in multidisciplinary teams on new indication areas.
  • Extended Kaleido’s in vitro screening platform for higher throughput and richer data, and wrote the R package (phgrofit) to model kinetic pH and OD600 curves at scale.
  • Analysed shallow shotgun sequencing, 16S rRNA sequencing and kinetic plate reader data in R and Tableau.
  • Adapted flow cytometry for targeted study of the microbiome, and ran 16S rRNA gene sequencing on Illumina MiSeq from DNA extraction onward.

University of Virginia, Charlottesville, VA

2015 to 2016
Laboratory Technician II, Derré Lab
  • Ran a high-content microscopy screen for host genes needed in the Chlamydia trachomatis developmental cycle, and contributed experiments to two publications.
